Sticky Wall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

In one line: your notes stay on your phone. Sticky Wall has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours, and we never see what you write.

What stays on your phone

  • Notes, spaces, checklists, drawings, stickers, reminders and the photos you attach.
  • Your settings, and anything in Archive or Recently deleted.
  • Reading writing out of a photo happens on the phone, with a model included in the app. The picture is not uploaded.

Nothing in this list is sent to us, because Sticky Wall has no server to send it to.

Things that leave your phone only when you ask

  • Backups. "Back up to a file" writes one file where you choose, which may be a cloud service such as Google Drive. That copy is then handled by whatever service you picked.
  • Sharing. Sharing a note as a picture, or a wall as a picture or PDF, sends it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet.
  • Crash reports. If the app closes unexpectedly, the technical details are written to a file on your phone. They are sent only if you tap "Send the last crash report" and pick an app to send them with.

Google Play billing

Sticky Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and tells the app whether the purchase is active. We never receive your payment details.

Advertising

There is none. Sticky Wall contains no advertising library at all, so no ad can be shown and no advertising identifier is read from your device. This is true of the free version as well as Sticky Pro.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications — to show your reminders and the end of a focus session.
  • Alarms & reminders — so reminders arrive at the minute you chose.
  • Photos you pick — only the pictures you choose are copied into the app. Sticky Wall does not browse your gallery.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock — used only to unlock the app or a locked space. Sticky Wall never stores a passcode of its own.

Children

Sticky Wall is a general-audience notes app and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete a note, then empty Recently deleted, and it is gone from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.
